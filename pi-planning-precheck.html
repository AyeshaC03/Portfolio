A deterministic SAFe-oriented aid for surfacing team capacity pressure and cross-team dependency gaps before Day 1. The sample plan below is fictional and illustrative; use findings to prepare conversations, not to make PI commitments.

Who & whenScrum Masters, RTEs, or pre-PI facilitators reviewing the plan before PI Planning Day 1.

Case study / operating brief

Problem

Capacity overload, unplanned provider work, and timing or confirmation gaps can surface during PI Planning and consume the room’s planning time.

Intended outcome

Move conflict discovery before Day 1 so facilitators can resolve assumptions and prepare the planning conversation. No measured savings or real-team result is claimed.

Operating assumptions

One facilitator maintains team capacity, prioritized items, iteration allocations, dependency need-by and provider iteration, and confirmation state.

Decision heuristics

Over 100% of a team’s iteration capacity is overcommitted. From 85% to under 100% is a tight-utilization watch. An unplanned, late, unconfirmed, or capacity-pressured dependency needs review.

Limitations

Manual inputs only; no Jira/ADO sync, calendars, forecasting, shared database, or AI. A finding is a prompt to verify, not a commitment decision or substitute for PI Planning.

Unit discipline: Capacity and planned demand use synthetic capacity-days. Compare them only within the same team. Do not add or compare story points, capacity-days, or other estimates across teams. All generated example data is fictional and illustrative.
